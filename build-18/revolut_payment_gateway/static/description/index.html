Accept Revolut payments in Odoo

Hosted checkout for cards, Revolut Pay, Google Pay and Pay by bank — built by an official Odoo partner

Take live payments through your Revolut Merchant account directly from Odoo — on the eCommerce checkout and on the customer invoice portal. Customers are redirected to Revolut's secure hosted page, so card data never touches your Odoo database. Secure by design

Payments use Revolut's PCI-compliant hosted checkout, so card data is handled by Revolut and never reaches your Odoo instance — keeping you in the lowest PCI scope (SAQ-A). Webhook notifications are cryptographically signature-verified with a timestamp tolerance and secret-rotation support.

  • PCI-compliant hosted checkout — card data handled by Revolut
  • HMAC-SHA256 signed webhooks with timestamp tolerance
  • Idempotent event processing — no double captures
  • Secret rotation supported without downtime
